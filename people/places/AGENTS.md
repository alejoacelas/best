# People by place

Start with [README.md](README.md).

When a city is added to [`other/places/travel-log.md`](../../other/places/travel-log.md),
create or refresh its page here. Before the visit:

1. Preserve Alejo's known people as anchors, whether or not they belong in
   [`../discover/`](../discover/).
2. Review [`../../work/calls/PLACES.md`](../../work/calls/PLACES.md) inside the private
   call repo. Treat call-derived locations as leads; verify them publicly before copying
   them here.
3. Ask each trusted anchor for one or two introductions. Record named referrals in
   [`../discover/sources.md`](../discover/sources.md) until meeting status, current place,
   and a strong sample of their work are checked.
4. Search the anchors' collaborators, local events, and communities. Never infer close
   friendship from an online mention.
5. Recheck location evidence older than 90 days for people being contacted. Mark people
   who divide time across cities as intermittent.
6. End with at most five priority contacts and a concrete next action for each.
7. Update [`reproduce/README.md`](reproduce/README.md) when a city map changes.
