# Discover people

See [README.md](README.md).

When adding someone:

1. Confirm Alejo has not met them. Keep uncertain names in `sources.md`, not the roster.
2. Record their current city or metro area and link the evidence. Use `Unknown`, never an
   inference from an employer or school. Add the date because locations change.
3. Link their personal site or blog, LinkedIn when useful, and one strong place to learn
   what they do.
4. Record who or which list led to them. Prefer the nearest human source over an algorithm
   or search result.
5. Preserve the README’s outer AI-origin tags and update `reproduce/README.md` when the
   roster or source inbox changes.
